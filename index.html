RULES

Goal

Be the first player to collect 10 Nectar Points and bring them to your home honeycomb.

Turn Sequence

1. The Weather Dice rolls. Based on the result, Nectar Tokens appear on the board.

2. You must move at least one of your bees one field. Each bee may move one field per turn.

3. When done, press Finish Turn.

Nectar Fields & Dice

● 1 Black fields: a roll of 1 places a 1-point token there.

◆ 2 Gold fields: a roll of 2 places a 2-point token there.

✦ 4 Pink/Center fields: a roll of 4 places a 4-point token there.

Rolls of 3, 5 or 6 place nothing. A bee standing on a field blocks new tokens there.

Carrying & Scoring

A bee picks up a Nectar Token simply by moving onto that field. Carrying a token makes you vulnerable to capture. Return to your home honeycomb to permanently bank your tokens.

Boost

Spend a Nectar Token as fuel: the bee gains movement equal to that token's value. Use the Boost button in the bee panel.

Chain

Adjacent friendly bees can pass tokens between them without using any movement. Click the Chain button, then click the target bee.

Blockade & Capture

A bee without a token cannot be attacked — use them as blockades. A bee with tokens can be attacked: the attacker steals all tokens and sends the defender home.

Multiplayer (4–6 Players)

Only 2 bees per player. The 1-token and 2-token fields are combined into a single split field — both token types drop there on rolls of 1 or 2.

Winning

First player to reach 10+ points wins. Play continues for 2nd and 3rd place if desired.

HOW TO PLAY
Sting or get stung

The Goal

Collect 10 nectar points and carry them back to your own honeycomb corner. First colony home with 10 wins.

Every turn the weather dice drops nectar on the board: ● 1 ◆ 2 ✦ 4. A bee picks nectar up simply by moving onto that field.

A bee carrying nectar can be attacked — an empty bee cannot. Bank your nectar at home before someone steals it.

Controls

Tap your bee — selects it, reachable fields light up.

Tap a lit field — move there. One field per bee, per turn.

Tap an enemy carrying nectar — attack it, steal the nectar, then tap one of its home fields to send it back.

Tap the same bee a second time — opens its nectar panel: Boost (spend a token for extra moves) and Chain (pass a token to a neighbouring friendly bee).

Drag to rotate · pinch to zoom the forest.

Done moving? Hit Finish Turn.
